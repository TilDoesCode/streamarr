Your decisions (recorded)

Look: Aurora + Signal spec labels

Artwork-driven ambient backdrop and a colour per title, soft glass surfaces, Outfit + Figtree; Signal's monospace labels for resolution, HDR, codec, audio and playback method, and signal bars for health.

Real Liquid Glass on iOS

Native glass first (tab bar, headers, sheets, menus), then expo-glass-effect for our own surfaces and controls, @expo/ui glass containers only for morphing. No imitation on iOS.

One large-screen shell

After R2, TV, web desktop and tablet render the same layout: glass rail with brand and profile, a hero that follows the focused (TV) or hovered (web) title over the ambient backdrop, the same rows and cards, the same detail page and version panel, the same player overlay. Only the input differs; phones get the compact variant.

Out of scope this round

Running and visually checking iPhone, iPad and Apple TV, including Liquid Glass (need Xcode) · HDR, Dolby Vision, passthrough, 4K and memory on a 2 GB TV (need real hardware) · offline downloads (next update). All stay in docs/client/BACKLOG.md.
