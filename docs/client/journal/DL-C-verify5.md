## Code reading only, audit limitations and rejected counterexamples

**No additional code-only C5 finding is counted.** The AST audit is useful but does not establish the advertised semantic guarantee for every await/helper:

- `intent-audit.test.ts:102` parses **only `manager.ts`**. It does not traverse imports/call graphs. Its explicit method exemptions include endTask and cleanup/outbox/metadata helpers; return-await, function-end awaits, annotations and selected next statements are accepted without proving side-effect ordering.
- The retained `round5-audit.test.ts` executes the actual audit against an in-memory fixture with an imported awaited mutating helper and a terminal helper await; the audit returns no findings. This is a positive test of the audit's boundary, **not a new product regression test or proof of an imported-helper bug**. C5-01 is a stale snapshot and C5-03 is a scheduling exclusion; neither is cured by adding a syntactic post-await guard.
- An initial mock delayed the **application** of compensation Pause until after a newer Resume and could reverse it. The installed native engines serialize command application; this mock alone does not establish that ordering on a device. It was rejected as a counted defect and removed from the retained repros. Compensation remains covered by the old intent tests and source review; no native change is requested.
- The 30 s overshoot cap is an accepted decision: the boundary test permits a proof beginning at +30 and completing at +35, and rejects a proof beginning at +31. Repeated normal `playing` state, metadata/track events and first-frame events do not reset an established epoch in code; the maintained tests cover pause, background, network, reload, buffering and unsettled-start resets. No real media producing legitimate >30 s overshoot was measured, so no severity is assigned to that hypothetical limit.
- Only From beginning chosen over a saved position creates a pending zero target. Other fresh zero starts remain unchanged as explicitly accepted. The corrected null-floor, nonzero seek, at-most-two local reloads, local engine retention/no playback API requests and streaming C31 tests pass; C5-02 is the remaining provenance gap in the actual VLC adapter.
- Busy primaries use a real no-op handler, busy accessibility state and title/status label. Downloads row actions are **siblings** of the row's press target; detail controls have no pressable ancestor action. The no-op cannot call the caller's handler, and maintained TV list/detail verifying/pending tests pass. No device responder traversal was performed; no parent-press leak was established. de/en parity, new ICU parameters/plurals, literal scan and changed one-line comments pass.
