Play, scrubbing (tap and long press), panels, Menu chain, resume (also after terminate) and next episode work muted;
glass text measures >= 5:1; iPhone, iPad and Google TV show no regressions.

## Decisions
- Remote automation via WebDriverAgent (XCUIRemote) outside the repo; only the helper `docs/client/tv-remote.sh` is
  committed. argent stays for screenshots, describe, debugger and native view inspection.
- Apple TV leaves focus restore to UIKit/TVFocusGuideView (no JS restore) and keeps focus in the tab bar on tab switch.
- tvOS player route uses `presentation: 'card'` (fade) so react-native-tvos sees remote events; other platforms keep
  `fullScreenModal`. Menu in the player is routed through `usePreventRemove` (preventNativeDismiss) on Apple TV only;
  `TVEventControl.enableTVMenuKey()` had no effect against the native stack pop.
- The hidden player overlay stays focusable on Apple TV (alpha 0.011, box-none) instead of making the expo-video view
  unfocusable (expo views ignore pointerEvents; a wrapper with pointerEvents none did not stop UIKit focusing it).

- TV lists get an explicit height (VirtualizedList wraps them in a content-sized TVFocusGuideView); phones keep flex 1.

  is why Filme worked (chips under the pill) and Serien did not. Tried and rejected on device: a title-line guide with
  destinations (guide view itself became focusable: react-native-tvos makes a guide without autoFocus selectable once
  destinations are passed, before they resolve), a title-line autoFocus guide containing the pill (it captures Down/Right
  from the pill: Down from Beliebt stayed on Beliebt), a guide left of the pill (Up from chips still went to the tab
