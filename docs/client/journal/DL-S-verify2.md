| **V2-03** | **Major** | **Ambiguous idempotency key breaks tuple identity and upgrade.** Same viewer, `(deviceId:"alpha\|beta", clientRef:"gamma")` and `(deviceId:"alpha", clientRef:"beta\|gamma")` are distinct accepted tuples; both POSTs return the **same downloadId**.
