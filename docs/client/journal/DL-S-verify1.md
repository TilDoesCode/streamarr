## Behavior that passed and limits of the evidence

- **Ownership/auth:** another viewer got 404 for GET/renew/complete/delete of Anna's download; its list contained no Anna records/capabilities. Admin activity rejected viewer and anonymous callers with 401. A separate Anna session saw metadata but no file URL until it supplied the stable deviceId, as documented. Token generation is 32 cryptographically random bytes, hash-indexed for lookup and Data-Protection protected for recovery; no entropy defect was found.
- **HTTP and identity:** suffix `bytes=-101`, open range, last-37-byte range with upper bound `9223372036854775807`, and offsets near `Int64.MaxValue` behaved correctly. Multi-range was permissibly ignored with full 200. Unsatisfiable and zero-suffix ranges gave 416 plus `Content-Range: bytes */3819976`. Strong/mismatched/weak/date If-Range behavior, HEAD length/ETag/type/attachment, no-store/no-referrer and body bytes passed. The forced-reset builder check resumed successfully.
